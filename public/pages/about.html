About the MedShare Platform

Building a zero-waste, trusted healthcare ecosystem starting at KIET Group of Institutions, Ghaziabad.

Our Mission

MedShare was conceived with a straightforward realization: millions of households purchase full strips of medicines, consume two or three tablets, and store the rest until they expire. Meanwhile, low-income patients, migrant workers, and students right next door struggle with healthcare expenses.

MedShare bridges this disparity through safe, regulated peer-to-NGO matching. By ensuring every donation is in its original sealed blister packaging with at least 90 days of shelf life, we convert potential medical waste into community lifelines.

Technology Architecture

Frontend:
  • Pure Semantic HTML5, CSS3 with 6-color design tokens
  • Vanilla JavaScript (ES6+ Modules)
  • Centralized API client (no scattered fetch calls)
  • Full accessibility keyboard navigation & ARIA live regions
Backend & Database:
  • Node.js + Express.js modular REST API
  • MongoDB with Mongoose ODM & Compound Indexing
  • JWT authentication stored in secure httpOnly cookies
  • Bcrypt hashing & express-rate-limit protection
  • Multer photo upload validation (JPG/PNG/WebP, max 2MB)
