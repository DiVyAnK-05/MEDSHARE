How Verification Works

Every medicine on MedShare undergoes dual-stage algorithmic and human verification before distribution.

1

Donor Submission & Photo Audit

The donor submits the brand name, active salts, batch number, and high-resolution packaging photograph. The server checks that the medicine is not expired and has at least 90 days of shelf life remaining.

2

Volunteer Review & Approval

Trained volunteers inspect the batch number, verify tamper seals on the blister foil, and confirm no restricted substances (narcotics, cold-chain items) are listed. Once approved, the listing goes live in the public directory.

3

Community Matching & Request

A registered community member or patient requests the medicine. The system allocates stock and generates a unique tracking code (`REQ-2026-XXXX`). Both donor and receiver receive instant coordination notifications.

4

Physical Hand-over at KIET or Partner NGO

Medication is deposited at an authorized campus drop-off desk (e.g. KIET Gate 1 Helpdesk) or partner NGO center (e.g. Pehchaan Care Foundation), where a final physical check verifies seal integrity before handover to the patient.
