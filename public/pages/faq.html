Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about donating, requesting, and safety compliance on MedShare.

Can I donate partially used strips or cut tablets?

No. MedShare enforces a zero-tolerance policy against opened packaging. Only full, original, uncut sealed blister strips or factory-sealed bottles with visible batch numbers and expiration dates can be accepted to eliminate contamination risks.

Why is there a 90-day minimum shelf-life requirement?

Verifying the medicine, matching it with an in-need community member, and coordinating drop-off at an NGO desk takes time. A 90-day buffer ensures the recipient has ample time to complete their full treatment course safely before the medicine reaches its manufacturer expiration.

Are medicines free for receivers?

Yes, 100% free. MedShare is a humanitarian platform. Neither donors nor receivers are ever charged, and buying or selling donated medicines is strictly prohibited and leads to immediate account termination and legal reporting.

Where are the physical collection points in Ghaziabad?

Our primary pilot drop-off desk is at KIET Group of Institutions (Muradnagar, Ghaziabad) at the Main Gate Reception Desk. Additional community collection desks operate at partner centers such as Pehchaan Care Foundation in Raj Nagar Extension and Vasundhara Sector 10 Dispensary.

Is my personal phone number or address visible to the public?

No. Your privacy is protected by default. Public visitors can only see the medicine name, quantity, category, and broad locality (e.g. KIET Campus). Exact donor contact details are never exposed publicly and are shared only after an admin verifies the match.
