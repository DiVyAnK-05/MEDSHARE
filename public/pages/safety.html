Medicine Safety & Regulatory Protocol

Our strict standards guarantee that every redistributed medicine is unexpired, authentic, and completely safe.

⚠️ Mandatory Medical & Regulatory Disclaimers:

  • Never consume any medication without explicit doctor or licensed pharmacist advice.
  • MedShare is a community coordination platform and does NOT provide medical advice, prescriptions, diagnosis, or clinical treatments.
  • Always inspect packaging and physical expiry dates before administration.
  • In compliance with Indian pharmaceutical regulations (Drugs and Cosmetics Act), peer-to-peer redistribution is facilitated strictly through verified NGOs, institutional drop desks, and licensed pharmacists.

✓ Permitted for Donation (Subject to Audit)

  • ✅ Unopened, Sealed Blister Packs: Strips with complete foil intact and no cut margins.
  • ✅ Minimum 90 Days Shelf Life: Medicines with at least 3 months remaining before expiry date.
  • ✅ Legible Batch & Expiry: Printed details clearly legible on the foil or carton.
  • ✅ Over-the-Counter (OTC) Essentials: Paracetamol, antacids, ORS, multivitamin supplements.
  • ✅ First-Aid & Antiseptics: Sealed bandages, povidone-iodine ointments with intact tamper seals.
  • ✅ Stored Correctly: Kept in cool, dry conditions away from moisture or direct sunlight.

✗ Strictly Prohibited on MedShare

  • ❌ Expired Medicines: Any drug near or past its printed expiration date.
  • ❌ Opened, Cut, or Partial Strips: Loose tablets without sealed original blister pockets.
  • ❌ Cold-Chain Items (Insulin, Vaccines): Products requiring continuous 2°C - 8°C refrigeration.
  • ❌ Narcotics & Schedule X Drugs: Sedatives, sleeping pills, opioid pain relievers (Tramadol, Alprazolam).
  • ❌ Injectable Solutions & Infusions: Ampoules, IV fluids, or chemotherapy items.
  • ❌ Opened Liquid Syrups & Suspensions: Unsealed syrups susceptible to bacterial contamination.

The MedShare Verification Standard

1. Image Analysis & Optical Audit

Our verification volunteers cross-reference uploaded photographs against manufacturer packaging templates to identify counterfeit or damaged strips.

2. 90-Day Algorithmic Guard

Our server engine mathematically rejects any listing with less than 90 days of shelf life. This ensures ample buffer time for verification, collection, and patient use.

3. Controlled Drug Blacklisting

Automated token screening blocks prohibited salt names, psychiatric medications, and cold-chain biologics from entering the directory.

4. Physical NGO Hand-over Inspection

Before a requested medicine is released to a patient, volunteer desk coordinators at KIET or partner NGOs physically inspect the blister seal for pinhole punctures or discoloration.
