Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • MedShare Healthcare Data Protection Charter

1. Healthcare Data Minimization

MedShare collects only the bare minimum information required to securely coordinate medicine donations and drop-offs. We do not collect medical history, insurance numbers, or government health IDs.

2. Strict Contact Masking

Personal phone numbers, full names, and exact addresses are never visible on public directory pages. In the public medicine directory, only general localities (such as "KIET Campus" or "Vasundhara") are displayed.

3. Password & Session Security

All passwords are encrypted with bcrypt (10 rounds of salt) before database storage and are never retrievable in plain text. User sessions use JSON Web Tokens (JWT) stored exclusively in secure, httpOnly cookies protected against cross-site scripting (XSS).

4. No Commercial Sharing

MedShare is a non-profit college project. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your contact or donation records to third-party advertisers, insurance providers, or pharmaceutical corporations.
