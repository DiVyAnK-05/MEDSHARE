Help someone in the Ghaziabad community by listing your sealed, unexpired medicines.

Strict Safety Policy Before You List: 1. Original Sealed Packaging: Blister strips must be completely intact. Loose tablets or opened bottles are strictly prohibited.
2. Minimum Shelf Life: Medicines must have at least 90 days (approx. 3 months) remaining before expiry.
3. Prohibited Items: No narcotics, sleeping pills, cold-chain requiring items (insulin, vaccines), or hospital injections.
