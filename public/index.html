Bridging the Healthcare Divide in Ghaziabad

Every year, thousands of unexpired medicine strips are discarded after standard treatments, while nearby community members struggle with out-of-pocket healthcare expenses.

The Critical Problem

  • ❌ Household Medicine Waste: Up to 23% of purchased medicines expire in domestic medicine cabinets untouched.
  • ❌ Environmental Contamination: Flushed or landfill-discarded medicines seep into soil and local waterways.
  • ❌ High Out-of-Pocket Burden: Low-income households and students often delay vital treatments due to prescription costs.

The MedShare Solution

  • ✅ 100% Sealed & Verified: Every listing is examined by safety reviewers before appearing in the public catalogue.
  • ✅ Zero-Cost Redistribution: Donated medicines are redistributed completely free to verified individuals and NGO clinics.
  • ✅ Hyperlocal Drop Desks: Accessible collection points at KIET Campus and partner NGO centers in Ghaziabad.
Simple & Safe Process

How MedShare Works

A closed-loop safety protocol ensuring that only authentic, undamaged, and unexpired medications reach recipients.

1

List Medicine

The donor enters medicine details, expiry date (≥ 90 days), and uploads a photo of the original sealed blister pack.

2

Safety Verification

Our verification team audits the batch number, sealed packaging, and verifies no controlled substances are listed.

3

Community Request

Verified medicines appear in the browse directory. Registered receivers submit requests specifying their requirement.

4

Safe Hand-Over

Donation is dropped off at a partner NGO desk or KIET Campus collection point for secure dispensing.

Active Pilot Scope

KIET Ghaziabad Pilot & Scalability Roadmap

MedShare is deployed as an active pilot at the KIET Group of Institutions (Muradnagar, Ghaziabad), leveraging campus hostels and local community networks across Raj Nagar Extension, Vasundhara, and Indirapuram.

1
Phase 1 (Current): KIET & Ghaziabad Pilot

Campus student drop-off desks, partnered with Pehchaan Care Foundation.

2
Phase 2: Delhi NCR & Western Uttar Pradesh

Expansion to Noida, Greater Noida, and Meerut district health cooperatives.

3
Phase 3: All-India NGO Medicine Mesh

National network with smart GPS matching and pharmacy inventory synchronization.
